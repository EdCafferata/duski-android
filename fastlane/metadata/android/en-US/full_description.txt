Duski helps you fall asleep with a mix of calming, screen-free sounds — no ads, no distractions, just what you need to drift off.

FOR YOU
At the top you'll find a ready-made mix for your age, always free: womb sounds and shushing for babies, music box lullabies for kids, brown noise for teens, a distant thunderstorm for adults and Clair de Lune with soft rain for 65+ (with larger text). One tap and it plays.

CHOOSE YOUR SOUNDS
Choose from 29 sounds: nature sounds like rain, a distant thunderstorm, ocean waves, wind, crackling fire and a flowing stream, or gentle white-noise sounds like a fan, a vacuum cleaner, a passing train or an airplane cabin. For babies and little ones, add a soothing heartbeat, a womb sound, a parent's shush or music box lullabies (Brahms' Lullaby and Twinkle Twinkle).

CLASSICAL MUSIC FOR SLEEP
Drift off to beautifully generated classical pieces, including Bach's Prelude and Air, Pachelbel's Canon, Satie's Gymnopédie and Debussy's Clair de Lune — all rendered with Duski's own audio engine.

SLEEP TIMER
Set a sleep timer and Duski gently fades the sound out over the last moments, so it's never there to wake you back up.

SCREENSAVERS
While the sound plays, choose a calm, animated screensaver — drifting stars, gentle snow, sheep to count and more — perfect for a nightstand or a child's room.

SAFE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
Duski adapts to who's listening: pick an age group from baby to older adult and Duski automatically limits the volume to a safe, comfortable level.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Duski works fully offline with everything generated on your device — no account, no data collection, no ads.
